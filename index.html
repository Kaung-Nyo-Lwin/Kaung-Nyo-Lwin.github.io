Research

Small models, verifiable rewards.

L2BC (“Learn To Be Cool”) is my master’s thesis at AIT: a reinforcement fine-tuning framework for text-to-visualization in which every reward is verified by rules. Programs are executed, and their rendered charts are parsed and checked against ground truth, with no LLM or VLM judge in the loop.

  • GRPO with LoRA on Qwen2.5-Coder 1.5B and 3B, trained on 192 VisEval queries.
  • “Comfortability”, the verified reward’s expectation, sets the temperature, learning rate and reward shape of each epoch.
  • Decomposed rewards show what verification teaches, and where it stops: the one unrewarded aspect, sort order, degrades on unseen tasks.
Read the research

K. N. Lwin, T. Tangtortan, C. Silpasuwanchai. L2BC: Reinforcement Fine-Tuning for Text-to-Visualization with Rule-Based Rewards over Parsed Chart Schemas. Manuscript in preparation, 2026.
